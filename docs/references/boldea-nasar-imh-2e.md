# Boldea & Nasar — The Induction Machines Design Handbook, 2nd ed.

Ion Boldea and Syed A. Nasar, *The Induction Machines Design Handbook*,
2nd ed., CRC Press, 2010. ISBN 978-1-4200-6668-5.

Primary source for induction motor equivalent circuit physics used in
phasesweep: leakage inductances (Ch 6), magnetization curve (Ch 5),
steady-state equivalent circuit and R_fe (Ch 7), and losses (Ch 11).

| Chapter | Topic | Phase-sweep use |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| Ch 3 | Core loss material coefficients | Steinmetz/Bertotti parameters |
| Ch 5 | Magnetization curve and inductance | L_m, Carter K_c, saturation factor K_s |
| Ch 6 | Leakage inductances and resistances | Differential, slot, zig-zag, end-winding, skin effect |
| Ch 7 | Steady-state equivalent circuit | R_fe shunt placement, Thevenin breakdown, torque-slip |
| Ch 9 | Skin and on-load saturation effects | Deep-bar impedance, multilayer approach |
| Ch 11 | Losses | Core loss, stray load, surface, tooth pulsation |
