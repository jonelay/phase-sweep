# Uppsala 5 kW marine current generator

120-pole cable-wound SPM generator for hydrokinetic (tidal/marine
current) energy conversion. 2-metre stator diameter, 10 rpm nominal,
10.5 mm airgap. The most extreme design point in the validation set
(60 pole pairs, ~1 m bore radius, ultra-low speed).

## Sources

1. K. Thomas, M. Grabbe, K. Yuen, M. Leijon, "A Permanent Magnet
   Generator for Energy Conversion from Marine Currents: No Load and
   Load Experiments," ISRN Renewable Energy, 2012, Art. 489379.
   DOI: [10.5402/2012/489379](https://doi.org/10.5402/2012/489379)

2. M. Grabbe, S. Eriksson, M. Leijon, "On the Stator Slot Geometry of
   a Cable Wound Generator for Hydrokinetic Energy Conversion," The
   Scientific World Journal, 2015, Art. 812149.
   PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4386604/
