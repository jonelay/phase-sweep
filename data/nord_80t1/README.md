# NORD / Watt-Drive 80T1/4 — 4-pole IPM

IEC frame-80 interior-PM synchronous motor with strong saliency
(L_q/L_d = 2.11). The only motor in the dual-source frame-80 family
with a full published electrical datasheet.

## Source

NORD / Watt-Drive motor datasheet for 80T1/4 (Nr. 43931265).

## Notes

Companion WEG UE 921 motors share the frame but have different windings
and no independent electrical datasheet — see `data/weg_ue921/`.
